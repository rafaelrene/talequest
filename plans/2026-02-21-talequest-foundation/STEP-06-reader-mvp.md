# Step 06 - Reader MVP Features

## Objective
Deliver the first usable product experience for readers.

## Scope
- Story listing view.
- Story detail/reading view.
- Seed scripts with fixture stories (public by default).
- Read progress tracking for authenticated users.

## Why
- Establishes core value path before writer/admin complexity.

## Deliverables
- Seeded data can populate local environments quickly.
- End-to-end read flow works from list to detail.

## Acceptance
- Anonymous users can read seeded stories.
- Signed-in users can store/restore read progress.
